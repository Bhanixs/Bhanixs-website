Erthaloka Blockchain & Decentralised Infrastructure Web3 · IoT · AI

A planetary-scale technology backbone for climate action.

Four production modules unified under one platform: Web3 governance, NFT-based carbon credits, an IoT sensor mesh, and AI-driven sustainability reporting.

Year

Domain
Blockchain & Decentralised Infrastructure
The Challenge

Erthaloka needed a single technology backbone for climate action — DAO governance, a tradable carbon market, live environmental telemetry, and audit-ready reporting — all running in production, not as a pitch deck.

Solutions

We built a unified platform that puts governance, markets, sensors, and reporting under one roof:

01

DAO Governance

Token-weighted voting, treasury, and proposal lifecycle shipped as audited Solidity contracts.

02

NFT Carbon Market

Tokenized carbon credits with provenance, retirement, and on-chain settlement.

03

IoT Sensor Mesh

Custom firmware and an MQTT pipeline streaming environmental data from field to cloud.

04

AI Reporting

ML models turning raw sensor and satellite data into auditable sustainability reports.
