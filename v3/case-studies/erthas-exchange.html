ErthaExchange

A coin-powered marketplace for circular services.

A digital wallet system, service discovery, and user dashboard connecting a growing user base to hundreds of services from verified organisations.

Timeline
6 months
Year
2024
Domain
Blockchain & Decentralised Infrastructure
The Challenge

The Erthaloka ecosystem needed a real marketplace where its native coin could unlock tangible services — from co-living spaces to sustainable travel — with a trust layer of verified organisations and a seamless user experience.

Solutions

We built a full coin-powered marketplace with wallet, discovery, booking, and admin layers:

01

Digital Wallet

Secure coin wallet with top-up, balance tracking, and full transaction history.

02

Service Marketplace

Browse and book services across ten-plus categories from verified organisations.

03

User Dashboard

A personalised view of wallet, bookings, spending, and account status.

04

Admin Portal

A JWT-secured admin layer for managing the full platform ecosystem.
