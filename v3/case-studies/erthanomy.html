Erthanomy

A circular economy powered by on-chain infrastructure.

A complete on-chain economic layer: an ERC-20 utility token, DAO governance, DeFi lending, an NFT marketplace, and a DApp — built for the Erthaloka ecosystem.

The Challenge

The Erthaloka circular economy needed a native token and decentralised economic infrastructure — payments, governance, and DeFi — built transparently on-chain without relying on centralised intermediaries.

Solutions

We built the full on-chain economic layer for the ecosystem:

01

ERT Token

An ERC-20 utility and governance token with a transparent allocation model on Ethereum.

02

DAO Governance

A smart-contract-based proposal and voting system for token-holder governance.

03

DeFi Protocol

A microloan and borrowing protocol generating interest revenue on-chain.

04

DApp + NFT

A marketplace DApp with payments, subscriptions, and an integrated NFT engine.
