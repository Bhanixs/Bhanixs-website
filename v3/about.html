About Bhanixs

Built to build institutions.

Bhanixs was founded with one conviction: the businesses that will define the next decade are being built right now — and they need technology partners who think like co-founders, not contractors.

Founding Story

Bhanixs was founded as Xenveda Technologies three years ago in Bengaluru. We started with a simple belief: the best technology is built by people who understand the business it serves.

Three years. Multiple ventures co-built. Patents filed. A model proven.

We rebranded as Bhanixs — named for our founder, Bhandari — to reflect what we had become: not a service provider, but a technology institution building other technology institutions.

Today we operate across Bengaluru, Dubai, and Singapore, co-building technology that makes businesses impossible to compete with.
